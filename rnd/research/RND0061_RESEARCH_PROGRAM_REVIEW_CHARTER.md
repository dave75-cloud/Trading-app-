# RND-0061 — Research Program Review & Next-Generation Hypothesis Agenda

## Status
FROZEN REVIEW CHARTER — NON-TRADING / NO NEW STRATEGY TRIAL / NO PROSPECTIVE OUTCOME ACCESS

## Purpose
Conduct a project-level scientific review of the research program from RND-0034 through RND-0060Y and produce a ranked next-generation research agenda that can be pursued while the two active prospective evidence programs continue accumulating.

RND-0061 is a synthesis and prioritisation task. It does not test a new strategy, alter a frozen candidate, inspect prospective economic outcomes, consume reserved-final evidence, or grant trading authority.

## Core questions
RND-0061 must answer:
1. What has the project actually learned about intraday FX market structure and trading edge?
2. Which hypothesis families have been genuinely falsified under the governed development process?
3. Which findings are robust market-structure observations but not directional/trading edges?
4. Which promising mechanisms remain unresolved rather than disproven?
5. Which areas of hypothesis space remain materially unexplored?
6. What 3–5 independent research directions offer the highest expected scientific value during prospective accumulation?

## Evidence classes
The review must keep the following categories separate:

### A. Market-structure findings
Non-strategy facts or repeatable relationships in market data, e.g. activity-state or friction relationships.

### B. Strategy findings
Results tied to a specific directional/trading rule, candidate, threshold, session, or portfolio construction.

### C. Methodological findings
Lessons about backtesting, partitioning, gap semantics, costs, falsification, overfitting, multiple testing, and prospective validation.

### D. Engineering/governance findings
Capabilities and safeguards in the research, shadow, risk, execution, control, provenance, audit, and readout infrastructure.

### E. Open hypotheses
Mechanisms not yet adequately tested, provided they are genuinely independent of closed failed trials and frozen prospective candidates.

## Scientific boundaries
The review must NOT:
- inspect Q003 prospective economic outcomes;
- inspect RND-0060Q/R prospective economic outcomes;
- reuse 2021–2022 consumed validation as fresh evidence;
- open 2023–2024 reserved-final evidence;
- tune Q003;
- change the RND-0060P activity-policy cutpoint;
- rescue or invert closed RND-0060A–K or RND-0060T trials;
- choose pairs, times, signs, thresholds, or lookbacks post hoc from failed trial results;
- create broker-write, capital, live-execution, or promotion authority.

## Review method
For each important prior result, classify it as one of:
- ROBUST_POSITIVE_STRUCTURE
- PROMISING_BUT_UNCONFIRMED_STRATEGY
- VALIDATED_NEGATIVE / FALSIFIED
- MIXED / NON-MONOTONIC
- GOVERNANCE_OR_ENGINEERING_VERIFIED
- INCONCLUSIVE / STRUCTURALLY LIMITED

For each proposed future direction, score qualitatively on:
- independence from prior failed/frozen hypotheses;
- economic plausibility;
- data availability within permitted development evidence;
- susceptibility to transaction costs;
- degrees of freedom / overfit risk;
- falsifiability under one declared trial;
- potential portfolio diversification value;
- ease of preserving prospective integrity;
- expected information gain even if the hypothesis fails.

## Deliverables
RND-0061 must produce:
1. an authoritative scientific state-of-project summary;
2. a hypothesis-space map: closed, supported, unresolved, unexplored;
3. a list of major lessons learned;
4. a ranked 3–5 direction next-generation research agenda;
5. explicit exclusions / ideas not worth pursuing now;
6. a recommended sequence for the next research cycle;
7. a clear statement of what remains blocked pending prospective evidence.

## Promotion / execution boundary
RND-0061 produces research priorities only.

It cannot:
- freeze a new trading candidate;
- open validation;
- open reserved final;
- authorize shadow deployment;
- authorize capital;
- authorize broker writes;
- authorize live execution;
- merge automatically.

All such gates remain HUMAN_ONLY and separate.
