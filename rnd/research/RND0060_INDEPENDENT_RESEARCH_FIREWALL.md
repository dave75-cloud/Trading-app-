# RND-0060 — Independent Research Firewall

Status: CONTRACT FROZEN / DEVELOPMENT DATA ONLY

## Purpose

Permit a genuinely independent research program to progress while Q003 prospective evidence accumulates, without contaminating Q003 or reusing consumed/sealed evaluation data.

## Data allowed

- development evidence only through the frozen development boundary `2020-12-31T19:15:00Z` exclusive;
- immutable historical evidence already admitted to development by RND-0037;
- derived development-only features declared prospectively inside a child task.

## Data prohibited

- consumed 2021–2022 validation for candidate discovery, tuning or ranking;
- reserved-final 2023–2024 evidence;
- post-freeze Q003 prospective evidence from 2026 onward;
- Q003 validation outcomes or pair rankings as selection signals.

## Scientific rules

- each child hypothesis must be predeclared before execution;
- search space and trial count must be bounded and recorded;
- failed/null trials are retained;
- no pair dropping or weighting based on held-out outcomes;
- no nearby-threshold continuation of the exhausted M005 absolute-volatility line;
- mechanisms should be materially distinct from merely retuning Q003/R000 thresholds;
- multiple-testing burden and selection path must be recorded;
- any future candidate requires its own untouched validation plan.

## Initial research families permitted for design review

- regime-state transitions unrelated to absolute volatility threshold retuning;
- cross-sectional/cross-pair information with causally available inputs;
- intraday microstructure/friction-state mechanisms distinct from the Q003 ratio threshold;
- persistence/mean-reversion structure with predeclared state definitions;
- portfolio-level allocation hypotheses evaluated only after pair-level mechanics are frozen.

No family is authorized for execution until a child task freezes its exact hypothesis, features, parameters, search space, falsification rule and evidence boundary.

## Authority

No broker writes, prospective validation access, reserved-final access, candidate promotion, capital allocation or merge authority.
