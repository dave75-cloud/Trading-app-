# RND-0039 — Higher-Volatility Eligibility Hypothesis Falsification

Status: **PREDECLARED / OUTCOMES NOT AUTHORIZED**

## Purpose

RND-0039 tests a hypothesis generated by the RND-0035 development robustness study: that restricting otherwise-fixed M005 entries to higher realized volatility may improve signal-to-friction economics by excluding low-amplitude opportunities whose expected move is small relative to execution costs.

This is a hypothesis-falsification task, not a strategy-selection task. It must not treat the prior A016 result as a promoted strategy.

## Evidence scope

Development only:

- start inclusive: `2015-01-01T00:00:00Z`
- end exclusive: `2020-12-31T19:15:00Z`
- symbols: AUDUSD, EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY
- timeframe: M5
- evidence basis: governed RND-0037 24/24 pair-year development assembly

Validation and reserved-final evidence remain closed.

## Fixed strategy semantics

All R000 semantics remain fixed except the volatility eligibility threshold under the explicitly declared hypothesis comparisons:

- fast MA 20
- slow MA 50
- volatility window 12
- population standard deviation (`ddof=0`)
- signal delay 1 eligible observed bar
- minimum hold 3 observed bars
- frozen sessions unchanged
- bid/ask execution unchanged
- gap semantics unchanged

## Predeclared comparison set

The task may compare only:

1. R000 reference threshold `0.0005`;
2. predeclared higher-volatility threshold `0.0006` (the former A016 condition), relabeled only as the hypothesis treatment and **not** as a selected strategy.

No threshold above `0.0006` may be tested.
No new intermediate thresholds may be added.
No threshold may be tuned by pair.
No session, MA, delay, hold, gap, or latency parameter may vary jointly with the threshold.

This two-point comparison is chosen specifically to test whether the previously observed direction persists over the now-complete development period, not to optimize the threshold.

## Primary falsification questions

RND-0039 must answer:

1. Does the 0.0006 eligibility condition improve full-development net economics relative to R000 consistently across pairs?
2. Is any improvement present in 2020, which was not included in the original RND-0035 threshold observation?
3. Is any improvement mainly explained by fewer trades and lower execution-cost exposure?
4. Does the higher threshold improve gross economics as well as net economics, or only reduce cost drag?
5. Does concentration by year or pair remain excessive?
6. Does dependence-aware bootstrap evidence materially improve relative to R000?
7. Does the four-pair equal-unit concurrent reference improve without relying on one pair or one year?

## Required outputs

If separately authorized, produce:

- per-pair R000 and 0.0006 outcomes over 2015–2020;
- absolute and relative differences in trade count, gross equity, net equity, max drawdown, hit rate and execution cost drag;
- 2020-only treatment-vs-reference contribution;
- pair/year and pair×year concentration for both arms;
- cost bridge and break-even diagnostics for both arms;
- frozen stationary-bootstrap diagnostics for both arms;
- observed equal-unit concurrent reference for both arms;
- explicit statement of whether the hypothesis is supported, weakened or falsified.

## Interpretation constraints

- A positive result does not authorize promotion.
- A negative result should close the higher-volatility lead unless a new independently justified hypothesis is predeclared.
- No multiple-threshold search is permitted.
- No validation/final evidence may be opened because of RND-0039.
- No pair-specific rule may be created from these outcomes.
